Set up once. Runs forever.

The HubSpot integration takes minutes to connect and requires no ongoing maintenance — it just works in the background as your team closes deals.

01

Connect Your HubSpot Account

Authenticate your HubSpot portal from the Know Why integrations dashboard. We use OAuth — no API keys to manage, no developer needed.

02

Choose which deal stage changes fire an interview — typically Closed Won and Closed Lost. You can filter by pipeline, deal owner, or deal size.

Once a buyer completes their interview, the AI-analyzed summary and structured win/loss data are written back to the HubSpot deal record in real time.
